About My University

Rabindranath Tagore University

Why We Created This Website

My University is an independent educational platform created for the students of Rabindranath Tagore University (RTU).

Our purpose is to make academic resources easily accessible in one place, including previous year question papers, study notes, university notices, results and other educational information.

Educational Purpose

This website is created only for educational and informational purposes. It is not intended to promote, support or participate in illegal, misleading or harmful activities.

Copyright & Disclaimer

We respect copyright and intellectual property rights. We aim to share original, authorized or publicly available educational resources. We do not support piracy or unauthorized distribution of copyrighted materials.

My University is an independent student initiative. It is not the official website of Rabindranath Tagore University and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the university.

Always verify important announcements through the university's official website.

Our Goal

To make learning resources easier to access for every RTU student.
